On a ₹40,000 net monthly salary, you qualify for a home loan of roughly **₹22.6 lakh**. That's at standard bank assumptions: 50% FOIR cap, 8.75% per annum interest, 20-year tenure, and no existing EMIs. Already paying off a car loan or personal loan? That gets subtracted first.

## The exact math for ₹40,000 salary

Banks cap total EMI burden at 50% of net monthly income, called FOIR (Fixed Obligations to Income Ratio). On ₹40,000, that's ₹20,000 available for EMI. Reverse-calculate that EMI into a loan amount at 8.75% over 20 years and you land at ₹22.6 lakh.

```
Eligible EMI = ₹40,000 × 50% = ₹20,000
Loan amount at 8.75% / 20 yrs = ₹20,000 × 113.16 = ₹22,63,184
```

| Existing EMI | Eligible EMI left | Home loan eligibility |
|---|---|---|
| ₹0 | ₹20,000 | ₹22.6 lakh |
| ₹4,000 | ₹16,000 | ₹18.1 lakh |
| ₹7,000 | ₹13,000 | ₹14.7 lakh |
| ₹10,000 | ₹10,000 | ₹11.3 lakh |

A ₹7,000/month car EMI cuts your home loan eligibility by roughly ₹7.9 lakh. Worth clearing before you apply, if the timeline allows it.

## How tenure changes the number

| Tenure | EMI per lakh | Loan eligibility on ₹20,000 EMI |
|---|---|---|
| 15 years | ₹999 | ₹20.0 lakh |
| 20 years | ₹884 | ₹22.6 lakh |
| 25 years | ₹822 | ₹24.3 lakh |
| 30 years | ₹787 | ₹25.4 lakh |

Stretching from 20 to 30 years adds close to ₹2.8 lakh of eligibility on the same EMI budget, though total interest paid rises.

## Two ways to raise this number

**Add a co-applicant.** A working spouse earning ₹30,000 as co-borrower pools income for FOIR, taking combined eligible EMI from ₹20,000 to ₹35,000. That alone pushes eligibility from ₹22.6 lakh to roughly ₹39.6 lakh.

**Extend the tenure.** A 25-year tenure lowers the EMI per lakh, so the same ₹20,000 budget qualifies for a larger loan than a 20-year one, at the cost of more total interest over the life of the loan.

## Frequently asked questions

### How much home loan can I get on a ₹40,000 salary?

Around ₹22.6 lakh, assuming 50% FOIR, 8.75% interest, a 20-year tenure, and no existing EMIs. It drops proportionally with any existing loan EMI.

### Is ₹40,000 salary enough for a home loan?

Yes, ₹40,000/month comfortably qualifies for a real home loan, roughly ₹22.6 lakh at standard terms, enough for a decent flat in a tier-2 city or an entry property in a bigger one, especially with a down payment.

### What down payment do I need on a ₹22.6 lakh home loan?

For properties up to ₹30 lakh, banks typically finance up to 90% (LTV), so budget around 10% down, roughly ₹2.5 lakh on a ₹25 lakh property, plus stamp duty and registration separately.
